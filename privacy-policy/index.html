Privacy Policy

Jebal Marketing respects the privacy of its clients, website visitors, and users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect information when providing our digital marketing, advertising, analytics, and technology integration services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information provided directly by clients or users, including names, email addresses, business contact information, company information, and information necessary to provide our services.

When authorized by a client, we may also process advertising and analytics data associated with advertising platforms and business accounts.

2. Advertising Platform Data

Jebal Marketing may integrate with third-party advertising platforms, including TikTok and other advertising and analytics services.

When properly authorized by the client, we may access and process advertising-related data for purposes including:

  • Advertising account integration
  • Campaign management
  • Campaign performance analysis
  • Conversion measurement
  • Advertising analytics
  • Performance reporting
  • Campaign optimization

We only access advertising accounts and related information when authorized to do so by the account owner or an authorized representative.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide digital marketing and advertising services
  • Manage authorized advertising integrations
  • Analyze campaign performance
  • Measure conversions
  • Generate reports
  • Optimize advertising campaigns
  • Provide client support
  • Maintain the security and functionality of our services

4. Data Sharing

Jebal Marketing does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with technology providers, advertising platforms, analytics providers, or other service providers only when reasonably necessary to provide authorized services or comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, meet legitimate business requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Third-Party Platforms

Our services may integrate with third-party platforms such as TikTok. The processing of information by those platforms is also governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

8. User and Client Rights

Clients and users may contact us regarding questions, access requests, correction requests, or deletion requests relating to information processed by Jebal Marketing, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, integrations, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
